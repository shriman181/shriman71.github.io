The Founder

Saksham Jain
Founder & CEO, Tavren
"The franchise industry runs on blind trust. Franchisors hope operators follow the rules. We replace hope with evidence."

Saksham Jain founded Tavren after observing firsthand the operational opacity that plagues multi-unit franchise brands. Having spent years studying the unit economics of distributed retail, he recognized that the intelligence gap between corporate and operator wasn't a technology problem — it was an architectural one. No one had built the layer that sits between raw operational data and the decisions franchise leadership actually needs to make.

Tavren is Saksham's answer to that gap: a purpose-built intelligence platform that gives franchise brands the visibility they've always lacked — without asking them to rip out the systems they already use.
